Welcome

Private tours that feel like travelling with family.

Candy Travels India is based at Grandthum in Greater Noida West. We plan group, family and outstation journeys with a focus on comfortable private travel and thoughtful itineraries.

Our own luxury 16-seater Force Urbania is our signature vehicle for keeping everyone together. For smaller parties and intercity or outstation plans, our booking partners can also arrange Innova Crysta, Ertiga, Swift Dzire and Hyundai Aura cars.
