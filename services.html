Services / Booking

Comfortable journeys, planned around your group.

Our own Force Urbania 16-seater is the signature choice for group travel. For smaller parties and intercity or outstation journeys, our booking partners can also arrange a car to suit your plans.

Groups

Friend & office groups

Private 16-seater hire for 8–16 people. Shared cost becomes simple: one vehicle, one driver, one itinerary. Popular for birthdays, alumni meets and offsites.

Families

Joint family holidays

Three generations in one cabin. We allow extra stop time, help with temple queues, and keep the AC and seating comfortable for long Yamuna Expressway or hill climbs.

Outstation

Plains to the hills

Mathura–Vrindavan darshan, Khatu Shyam and Kainchi Dham pilgrimages, Agra–Bharatpur heritage and nature trips, and hill breaks in Mussoorie, Nainital, Lansdowne, Shimla and Manali. Pickup is planned across Delhi NCR.

Additional booking partners

More ways to travel for smaller groups.

Booking steps

  1. Send dates, destination and group size on the enquiry form or WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm Urbania availability and share a kilometre / night outline.
  3. Lock the vehicle with a token; receive driver and pickup details.
  4. Travel together; drop back at your Noida Extension address.

Typically included

  • Private Force Urbania with experienced driver
  • Fuel as per the agreed route
  • Door pickup in Greater Noida West
  • Parking and tolls can be bundled or billed as actuals — we say which, before you pay
